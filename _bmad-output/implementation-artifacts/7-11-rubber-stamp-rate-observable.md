# Story 7.11: Rubber-stamp-rate observable

Status: done

<!-- Validation: create-story checklist applied 2026-06-02. -->

## Story

As a tenant administrator,
I want the rubber-stamp approval rate observed,
so that approval-fatigue degradation triggers a tuning revisit.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Given approval **decisions** recorded against `approval-required` AI actions, when measured over a **rolling 7-day** window, then the **rubber-stamp fraction** — the share of approvals decided within **< 5 s** of being requested — is **observable** both **per tenant** and **per `(tenant × reviewer)`**. "Approval" = an `AiActionApprovalDecisionRecorded` whose `DecisionKind == Approved` and whose originating `AiActionApprovalRequested.AiRiskClass == AiActionRiskClass.ApprovalRequired` (rejections, revision-requests, cancellations, and `low-risk` actions are excluded from the denominator). The fraction is **computed server-side**, is **deterministic and pure** given the injected `ISystemClock` and the tenant-bound decision snapshot, and is keyed strictly by the tenant ref from the authenticated binding (per reviewer = the safe `DecisionActorId` token). [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md#Story 7.11`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md#NFR46`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Governance/AiMediation/AiActionApprovalEvents.cs`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Contracts/Enums/AiActionRiskClass.cs`]

2. Given each approval decision, when its **decision latency** is computed, then latency = `DecidedAtUtc − RequestedAtUtc`, **server-measured in UTC**, **clamped ≥ 0** (a non-positive or future-skewed pair clamps to 0 s), reusing the `EscalationPolicyEvaluator.EffectiveAgeSeconds` / `NotificationThrottleEvaluator` server-measured-duration discipline exactly. A decision is **rubber-stamp** when `latency < 5 s` — **strictly less than** the rubber-stamp constant (exactly 5.000 s is **not** rubber-stamp). The **7-day rolling window** is keyed on `DecidedAtUtc` and measured against the injected clock: a decision is in-window when `now − DecidedAtUtc ∈ [0, 7 days)` (a decision exactly 7 days old is **outside**; future-dated decisions are ignored), mirroring `NotificationThrottleEvaluator.CountInTrailingWindow`. The boundaries are decided and tested explicitly. [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md#NFR46`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Notifications/NotificationThrottleEvaluator.cs`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Notifications/EscalationPolicyEvaluator.cs`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Audit/ISystemClock.cs`]

3. Given the **tenant-level** rubber-stamp fraction over the rolling 7-day window, when it **exceeds 15 %** (NFR46), then the **FR41 approval-tuning revisit condition is triggered and recorded** (AC5/AC7). "Exceeds" is **strictly-greater-than** evaluated with **exact rational/decimal arithmetic** (no floating-point compare): the condition fires when `rubberStampCount × 100 > 15 × totalApprovals` (equivalently `fraction > 0.15`). Exactly 15.000 % does **not** trigger; just above does. The boundary is decided and tested. The **denominator is the tenant-aggregate** count of qualifying approvals (NFR46 is tenant-scoped: "> 15 % of approvals in rolling 7 d"); per-reviewer fractions remain observable (AC1) and are carried in the recorded trigger for diagnosis, but the FR41 revisit condition itself is the **tenant-level** crossing. [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md#Story 7.11`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md#NFR46`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md#FR41 (line 90)`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Projections/ApprovalPriorityScorer.cs`]

4. Given a **zero or degenerate denominator**, when no qualifying approvals exist in the window, then the fraction is **undefined → treated as 0 / not-fatigued** (`0/0` never triggers; a single approval window is handled without divide-by-zero), and **no** revisit condition is recorded. Aggregation is keyed strictly by `(tenant-ref-from-authenticated-binding)` and `(tenant × reviewer)` — the tenant comes from the authenticated gateway binding carried on the decision snapshot, **never** from proposal/project/command refs, reviewer-supplied data, or correlation ids — so one tenant's approvals can never trigger, suppress, inflate, or leak into another tenant's rate, and a decision redacted-from-tenant-`B` is never counted in tenant-`A`'s rate. Decisions with a null/blank `DecisionActorId` are excluded from per-reviewer attribution (no phantom reviewer) but still count toward the tenant aggregate; this exclusion is stated in completion notes. [Source: `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture.md#API & Communication Patterns`; `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md#NFR2`; `src/Hexalith.ChatBot.Server/Gateway/Stages/ParticipantAuthorizationStage.cs`]

5. Given the rubber-stamp definition is an NFR46 **governance rule**, when the constants are defined, then the **< 5 s rubber-stamp latency**, the **15 % fatigue fraction**, and the **7-day rolling window** are modeled as **single-sourced governance constants** (e.g. `RubberStampRateObservable.RubberStampLatencySeconds = 5`, `FatigueFractionPercent = 15`, `RollingWindowDays = 7`), **not tenant-tunable knobs** — they are the *definition* of approval-fatigue under FR41/NFR46, not a tunable triage threshold like the Story 7.10 backlog cap. (Rationale: a tenant lowering the rubber-stamp definition is meaningless and a tenant raising it would *hide* fatigue, defeating the FR41 guard.) The constants are defined **once** and reused by the evaluator; no `TenantPolicyKnobType`, no `TenantPolicyValue` field, and no `TenantPolicySchema` validator are added. If — contrary to this decision — a tenant knob is judged necessary, it MUST follow the Story 7.10 closed-bounded-knob pattern exactly (closed schema, `Maximum` = the NFR46 governance value, lower-only, validator-guarded across all sibling validators, `range_invalid:`/`wrong_value_type:`/unknown-knob reason codes, safe-default fallback) — but the expected and recommended decision is **fixed governance constants**, stated in completion notes. ## Dev Notes

### Scope Boundaries

- Story 7.11 implements exactly **one** NFR46 mechanism: the **rubber-stamp-rate observable** — the share of approvals decided within **< 5 s** against `approval-required` actions over a **rolling 7-day** window, observable per tenant and per reviewer, with the **FR41 approval-tuning revisit condition** triggered and recorded when the tenant fraction **exceeds 15 %**. It is the **fifth** NFR46 anti-fatigue mechanism, layered on the clock-injected evaluator/coordinator + fail-closed-audit pattern shipped by Stories 7.7/7.9/7.10 and the AI-action approval events shipped by Epic 4.
- It builds directly on **Epic 4** (the `AiActionApprovalRequested`/`AiActionApprovalDecisionRecorded` events carrying `RequestedAtUtc`/`DecidedAtUtc`/`DecisionKind`/`DecisionActorId`/`AiRiskClass`, and the `AiActionRiskClass.ApprovalRequired` classification), **Story 7.7** (the pure clock-injected `EscalationPolicyEvaluator` + `EscalationEvaluationCoordinator` evaluate → per-event fail-closed audit → count pattern, `EffectiveAgeSeconds` server-measured duration, strictly-greater-than threshold), **Story 7.9** (the `NotificationThrottleEvaluator.CountInTrailingWindow` rolling-window discipline), and **Story 7.10** (the metadata-only enriched-result + fail-closed `*Fired` audit envelope + coordinator wiring, and the fixed-constant-vs-knob decision). **Reuse those; do not fork the evaluator discipline, the window/age math, the audit pattern, or the routing/redaction path.**
- The observable + revisit are **aggregate signals** keyed by tenant (and per reviewer for diagnosis). They must never become a covert channel for project/proposal content, evidence, command bodies, recipient PII, or AI payloads (NFR2). The trigger carries only counts, the derived rate, the safe reviewer refs, and the three governance constants.
- It does **NOT** implement the other NFR46 mechanisms (prioritization+grouping = 7.8; throttle/digest = 7.9; reviewer-backlog alert = 7.10 — all done) nor the **observable median/p95 time-in-queue per risk class** (that NFR46 dashboard clause belongs to Epic 8 / operational observability). Keep the evaluator shaped so Epic 8 dashboards can read the same per-tenant/per-reviewer aggregates.
- The **runtime/periodic evaluation caller** (Dapr timer / scheduled pass that materializes the decision snapshot from `ApprovalEventView` and invokes the coordinator) is **deferred** (consistent with 7.6/7.7/7.9/7.10). This story provides the deterministic evaluator, the metadata-only result + revisit record, the coordinator wiring, and the FR41 audit envelope — not an always-on `BackgroundService`.
