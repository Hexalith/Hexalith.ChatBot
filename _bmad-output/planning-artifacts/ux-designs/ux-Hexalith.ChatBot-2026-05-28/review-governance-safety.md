# Governance & Safety Review — Hexalith.ChatBot

## Overall verdict

**Broken.** The pair has a strong fail-closed posture and unusually good coverage of tenant-safe denial, actor labels, audit investigation, partial success, and accessible recovery. It is not yet a coherent implementation contract, however: one critical source contradiction can change whether boundary-crossing AI actions execute without approval, and several later binding PRD controls never reached the spines.

## Scope and strengths

This review tested `DESIGN.md` and `EXPERIENCE.md` against all four resolving frontmatter sources, the approved PRD addendum, and the Hexalith UX baseline. It focused on authorization and tenant isolation, redaction, AI proposal/approval boundaries, two-person administration, attribution and provenance, audit reconstruction, idempotency/retry, correction/recovery, evidence freshness, and UI/CLI/MCP parity.

The existing contract is strongest in these areas:

- Unauthorized and degraded states generally fail closed, preserve safe next actions, and avoid broad fallback access (`EXPERIENCE.md:61, 84, 106-107, 135-139, 291, 301, 311, 321, 329, 343, 351-355`).
- Actor type, permitted identity, source surface, correlation, policy, and outcome are kept close to conversation and audit events (`DESIGN.md:217, 227`; `EXPERIENCE.md:71, 73, 83, 174-181, 323-332`).
- Retry, duplicate suppression, partial success, terminal failure, and focus recovery are visible rather than collapsed into generic success or error (`EXPERIENCE.md:58-59, 82, 103-105, 123-139, 205-213`).
- Visual implementation correctly inherits FrontComposer and Fluent UI v5, uses semantic Fluent 2 tokens, and does not redefine the theme (`DESIGN.md:132-166, 231-239`; `EXPERIENCE.md:15-21`). This is consistent with `references/Hexalith.AI.Tools/hexalith-ux-instructions.md:3-40`.

## Findings

### Critical

- **[critical] Source contradiction: the same six boundary-crossing action classes are both necessarily approval-required and tenant-downgradable to low-risk.** The PRD defines `low-risk` as read-only and defines project mutation, file exposure, external send, task creation, tool invocation, and acting for a participant as `approval-required`; it also exposes `denied` and `unsupported` as user-facing risk outcomes (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1102-1120, 1215-1224, 1231-1237`). Yet the PM note permits tenant admins to “downgrade” those six classes, while the addendum gives those same classes `ai-action.low-risk-allowed` switches and says the classifier emits only `low-risk` or `approval-required` (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1233-1235`; `../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/addendum.md:25-33, 65-74`). The spines conservatively say risky requests create proposals and require confirmation, but never declare whether that rule overrides the policy switch or how `denied`/`unsupported` map around the two-output classifier (`DESIGN.md:219, 222-224, 238`; `EXPERIENCE.md:72, 77, 101, 148, 210, 334-355`). This is a **source contradiction**, not merely a spine omission; UI and backend teams can implement different approval boundaries while each believes it followed an authoritative source. *Fix:* establish one canonical evaluation order and one stable user-visible outcome taxonomy. Explicitly decide whether any of the six effect classes can ever bypass human approval; until that decision is approved, require approval for all six. Align the PRD glossary/table, FR41 note, Tenant Policy Schema, classifier output, and both spines, including mixed-risk behavior and denied/unsupported mapping.

### High

- **[high] Evidence freshness is visualized only as a color meaning; its fail-closed behavior is absent.** `DESIGN.md` says warning can mean stale evidence, but `Evidence chip`, `Evidence drawer`, `AI proposal panel`, the state tables, and the approval recovery row do not require a snapshot timestamp or `fresh` / `stale` / `expired` state (`DESIGN.md:147, 218-224`; `EXPERIENCE.md:75-81, 89-121, 205-213`). The source contract requires every evidence reference to carry freshness, forbids approval against expired evidence with reason `evidence-expired`, and also fails an ambiguous association decision closed when its evidence is beyond the freshness bound (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1382-1388, 1440-1441`). *Fix:* add freshness timestamp/state to the evidence component and every evidence-bearing surface; add refresh/re-evaluate behavior; make expired approval and association evidence a persistent blocked state with `approve`/`confirm` unavailable for `evidence-expired`; define the stale-but-permitted warning and audit outcome.

- **[high] Correction can appear complete before contaminated derived context is safe.** The spine says derived AI context is invalidated “where needed” or “where relevant” and jumps to `Corrected association` / `correction applied` (`EXPERIENCE.md:98, 114, 293-301`). The PRD requires the canonical `Correcting` and `Correction-delayed` states, acknowledgement from every affected derived store, blocked AI use until invalidation finishes, a p95 propagation target, and a P2 path on delay (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:435-451, 1322-1325`). *Fix:* add `Correcting` and `Correction-delayed` to state/component/feedback contracts; show predecessor/successor, per-store progress or safe summary, responsible owner, estimate and escalation; keep AI actions and affected context affordances blocked until all required invalidations acknowledge. A merged Conversation/Association surface is acceptable only if the IA maps it explicitly to PRD S4.

- **[high] Tenant administration omits the bounded-admin and two-person control.** Tenant Configuration and Nora's journey show one admin configuring confidence thresholds, low-risk policy, approval policy, service clients, and audit visibility, then succeeding; there is no proposed/pending-second-admin/rejected/expired/conflicted state or separation between aggregate operational visibility and per-project detail (`EXPERIENCE.md:43, 120, 303-311`). The source says tenant admin is not a superuser, security-sensitive knobs require a second admin and justification, admin assignment is audited, queue operations cannot mutate project records, and per-item details still require project authority (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1288-1300`; `../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/addendum.md:52-76`). *Fix:* add a bounded-admin component/flow and states for proposal, distinct second-admin approval, conflict, rejection, expiry/cancel, and activation; show proposer/approver, changed values, effective scope/time, justification, policy version, and audit link; make unavailable self-approval or insufficient-scope actions explainable without exposing project detail.

- **[high] The recommended denial copy and an Association Review state can disclose resource existence.** The “Do” example says, “You do not have access to this project,” and the surface contract names `unauthorized candidate suppressed` as a visible state (`EXPERIENCE.md:52-61, 111-116`). Both can distinguish an existing-but-forbidden target from a nonexistent or unmatched one, contrary to the spine's own `Unauthorized` rule and the PRD's explicit non-confirmation requirement (`EXPERIENCE.md:106`; `../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:689-714, 1356-1369`). *Fix:* replace the example with catalogued, existence-neutral language such as “Association could not be completed,” plus a safe request-access/escalate action. Collapse user-visible `unauthorized candidate suppressed` into an indistinguishable no-safe-result state; retain the precise suppression reason only in authorized audit/diagnostic evidence.

- **[high] Approval review does not expose enough authority and effect detail for an informed decision.** `AI proposal panel`, `Approval panel`, and Flow 8 include requester, project/files, recipient/destination, command, policy reason, and risk label, but omit the sender-authority class and delegation evidence, the classifier input tuple/version, policy snapshot ID, expected post-state, side effects, and audit events (`DESIGN.md:222-224`; `EXPERIENCE.md:79-81, 334-343`). Those fields are mandatory in the source's acceptance contract, and outbound authority can change between proposal and execution (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1231-1252`; `../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/addendum.md:124-149`). *Fix:* extend the proposal/approval components with all FR42 fields, show the exact sender-authority posture and originating requester, and define revalidation immediately before execution. A changed/revoked authority, changed policy snapshot, or changed effect set must block execution and require a refreshed proposal rather than silently reuse approval.

### Medium

- **[medium] AI-generated summaries lack a provenance and source-evidence distinction contract.** Actor badges distinguish AI actors, but neither spine defines the required `AI summary` label, model/version, generated timestamp, source-evidence IDs, nor source-evidence-default/collapse behavior (`DESIGN.md:214-217`; `EXPERIENCE.md:70-73, 174-180`). The PRD requires this distinction so an interpretation is not mistaken for authoritative mail/file/command evidence (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1198-1201, 1469-1472`). *Fix:* add an AI-summary component and behavioral row with the provenance string, explicit source links, non-color distinction, and source-first default; carry the same provenance into audit and exported/copied accessible representations.

- **[medium] Retry and duplicate-safety feedback lacks the per-operation conflict/replay contract.** The pair shows retry count, operation identity, duplicate-safety notes, and audit availability, but never states whether a repeat returns a prior outcome or is rejected as already decided/sent/corrected, nor does it expose an idempotency key or original-operation link (`DESIGN.md:220, 226-229`; `EXPERIENCE.md:58-59, 74, 82, 103-105, 128-137, 207-213, 313-321`). The addendum defines materially different replay windows and conflict responses for eight operation classes, and the PRD requires stable idempotency identifiers and audit presence (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/addendum.md:87-102`; `../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:1322-1329, 1372-1397, 1445-1449`). *Fix:* define a shared operation-status pattern that shows stable operation/idempotency identity, original outcome link, retry eligibility, attempt ceiling, and the exact safe replay/conflict result; prevent duplicate submission while pending and map every surface to the same stable status/reason codes.

- **[medium] UI/CLI/MCP parity is asserted, but the UX handoff does not map the complete parity set or MCP outcomes.** Command Surface Reference is described as explanatory, and Flow 6 proves only a CLI subset; MCP appears as an origin in the AI system flow but has no explicit parity/error/redaction mapping (`EXPERIENCE.md:44, 121, 240, 313-321, 345-355`). The source requires the full MVP operation set, one shared command spine, equivalent authorization/state/redaction outcomes, immutable origin attribution, and adapter-normalization tests (`../../prds/prd-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28/prd.md:169-175, 1307-1315, 1413-1420`). *Fix:* make Command Surface Reference link to the canonical parity-set/version and include an outcome matrix for UI/API, CLI, and MCP: command name, normalized input, authorization result, state transition, redaction/reason code, operation identity, audit origin, and long-running status. Add at least one MCP failure/recovery example; do not duplicate pipeline logic in the UI contract.

### Low

No low-severity findings. The remaining issues are either implementation-affecting or merely editorial and therefore omitted.

## Consistency notes

- All four `sources` entries in both spines resolve. The frontmatter also includes `prd-validation-report.md`, but that report validates the older 2026-05-10 PRD and should be labelled historical; its `overallStatus: Critical` is not a current verdict on the later approved PRD/addendum.
- The spines were last updated 2026-06-05, while the PRD addendum was approved 2026-06-09 and later binding UX elaborations were added. The missing correction, outbound-authority, cross-surface-attribution, admin, and freshness details are partly acknowledged in `m1-m2-surface-elaboration.md:30-50` and `implementation-conformance-addendum-2026-07-17.md:46-70`, but neither file appears in spine frontmatter and neither can silently repair a spine that claims closure (`EXPERIENCE.md:30, 46`). Distill the material controls into the peer spines or explicitly inherit those artifacts with precedence.
- The UX IA has nine named surfaces, while the PRD inventory has S1-S10 and later elaboration maps Correction, Outbound Approval, Cross-surface Attribution, and Admin Queue Operations into broader UX surfaces. Merging is defensible, but the mapping must be explicit in `EXPERIENCE.md`; otherwise source extraction can skip a load-bearing control while still accepting the IA-closure claim.
- Actor attribution and audit investigation are otherwise coherent and conservative: human, external, service, AI, background, CLI, MCP, and mailbox origins are distinguishable; system decisions are not disguised as chat; redacted audit detail has an escalation route. The findings above should extend this foundation, not replace it.
