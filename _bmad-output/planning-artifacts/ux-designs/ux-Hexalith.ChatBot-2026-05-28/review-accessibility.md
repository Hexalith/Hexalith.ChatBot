# Accessibility Review - Hexalith.ChatBot

## Overall verdict

The spine pair now meets a solid WCAG 2.2 AA behavioral floor for an enterprise SaaS operational tool. All three prior accessibility findings (touch-target thresholds, small-screen fallback, French expansion/truncation) are substantively closed with concrete, testable rules in `EXPERIENCE.md`. A handful of new gaps remain - the most material concern keyboard semantics for streaming AI output (interrupt control), explicit live-region politeness mapping for AI proposal/projection/rejection events, and missing accessibility coverage for PII redaction in conversation export.

## Status of prior findings

- **Touch-target thresholds** - closed. `EXPERIENCE.md:193` now states "touch-primary controls use at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels where layout allows; compact table/list controls must meet WCAG 2.2 AA target size with at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels or equivalent spacing from adjacent targets. Destructive and approval controls must not rely on compact-only sizing on phone or tablet." The same numbers are restated for the responsive surface at `EXPERIENCE.md:235`. Concrete, testable, and the destructive/approval carve-out blocks the obvious abuse case (shrinking Approve/Reject into a dense row on phone).
- **Small-screen fallback** - closed. `EXPERIENCE.md:233` documents the pattern explicitly: read-only summary + status, safe approve/reject/defer/confirm, copy/share handoff link, "open on larger screen" guidance, disabled dense editing with reachable (non-tooltip-only) explanation, preserved draft/filter state, and an explicit screen-reader equivalence sentence ("Screen reader users hear the same limitation, remaining actions, and recovery path from the surface heading or first blocked-state panel"). The Phone breakpoint row at `EXPERIENCE.md:229` links to this pattern.
- **Localization truncation** - closed. `EXPERIENCE.md:221` now enumerates: critical state and action words must wrap, use an approved short label, or move into labelled row detail before truncation; columns allowed to collapse first (raw IDs, secondary timestamps, low-priority metadata, repeated project/tenant context already visible in the surface header); columns that must remain visible or move to labelled row detail (actor, risk, state, confidence, next action, safe recovery reason). The "Avoid concatenated strings for accessible names and state descriptions" rule at `EXPERIENCE.md:220` covers the related screen-reader concern. Testable.

## Findings

- **medium** No explicit accessibility rule for interrupting/stopping a streaming AI response or AI proposal generation via keyboard. The spine acknowledges streaming text animation in reduced-motion (`EXPERIENCE.md:178`) but does not specify that a Stop/Cancel control must be keyboard-reachable while streaming, must occupy a stable focusable position (not appear/disappear inline and steal focus), and must announce cancellation through the live-region politeness defined in the state-to-feedback matrix. (Composer/action entry component, `EXPERIENCE.md:71`; Conversation Detail states, `:113`.) *Fix:* add a bullet under "Reduced motion and auto-scroll" or "Conversation and audit semantics" requiring an always-keyboard-reachable stop/cancel affordance during streaming, with a polite "Response stopped" announcement and stable focus return to the composer or AI proposal panel.
- **medium** Live-region politeness is defined at the state-family level (`EXPERIENCE.md:124-134`) but not pinned to the AI-specific events that arrive asynchronously: "AI proposal ready" (`:100`), "Command accepted, projection pending" (`:102`), and "Approval rejected" (`:101`). The decision log claims live-region behavior is covered, but the spine leaves a screen-reader user reliant on "appropriate live-region behavior" without saying which of these is polite vs assertive. The product is most likely to over-announce (chatty `aria-live="polite"` on every projection-pending tick) or under-announce (silent approval rejection on the user's own action). *Fix:* add a short table or row mapping AI proposal ready -> polite (one announcement on the user's own request), projection pending -> polite (one announcement, not repeated on each poll), approval rejected on the current user's submitted action -> assertive; rejection observed in a queue for someone else -> polite or no live announcement.
- **medium** PII redaction in conversation export is referenced as a product concern but has no accessibility rule. The spine repeatedly redacts unauthorized details on screen (`EXPERIENCE.md:60`, `:105`, `:194`, `:251`), but exports/copies, audit copy actions, and any "copy email body" affordance on attachment rows are not constrained. A screen reader announcing the unredacted text from a copied conversation, or a downloaded transcript containing identities the on-screen view suppressed, would break the tenant-isolation guarantee specifically for AT users. (Audit Investigation "export/copy unavailable" state, `EXPERIENCE.md:118`; Conversation Detail, `:113`.) *Fix:* add an Accessibility Floor bullet stating that any export, copy, or "read aloud" affordance must apply the same redaction the visual surface applies, and that the export's accessible name/description must not contain the redacted source text. Pair with a screen-reader-equivalent affordance for "this export is redacted; full detail requires escalation."
- **medium** The disabled-control explanation rule is strong (`EXPERIENCE.md:164`: "tooltip-only explanation is insufficient") but is undermined by the Approval controls component spec (`EXPERIENCE.md:79`: "Disabled controls explain missing permission or invalid state") which still allows a disabled button to be the explanation surface. Disabled buttons in Fluent UI v5 default to non-focusable; a screen-reader user tabbing through Approve/Reject/Revise/Cancel will silently skip the explanation. *Fix:* require that disabled approval/association/correction controls either remain focusable with `aria-disabled="true"` plus an announced reason, OR be paired with an adjacent, focusable "Why unavailable?" affordance. This just makes the existing "tooltip-only is insufficient" rule reach the focus order.
- **medium** No explicit rule on shortcut conflicts or single-key shortcuts. `EXPERIENCE.md:145` permits command palette and keyboard shortcuts for developers/operators but does not require WCAG 2.1.4 Character Key Shortcuts (single-key shortcuts must be remappable, disable-able, or only active on focus). Power-user keyboard shortcuts inside a chat composer are a known accessibility footgun for speech-input users. *Fix:* add a bullet to the keyboard/focus model requiring that any single-character or modifier-free shortcut is disabled by default inside text-entry controls and is remappable or globally disable-able in user settings; document where the toggle lives.
- **low** Landmarks are listed (`EXPERIENCE.md:159`: navigation, project context, main conversation/detail, complementary evidence/review panel, queue filters, status region) but no rule says repeated landmark roles must carry unique accessible names. Conversation Detail + Evidence drawer + AI proposal panel can all read as "complementary" to a screen reader. *Fix:* add a sentence requiring unique `aria-label` on repeated landmark roles within a single surface.
- **low** Skeleton/`aria-busy` rule (`EXPERIENCE.md:126`) does not say when `aria-busy` is cleared, or whether focus inside a busy region is preserved when content swaps in. Screen-reader users frequently lose place on skeleton -> real content swap. *Fix:* add a bullet requiring that `aria-busy` is cleared on the same node, focus is preserved or moved to a labelled landing point, and newly-loaded historical content does not announce.
- **low** The color-contrast table in `DESIGN.md:154-163` covers the required pairs but does not state which token pairs apply in dark mode or in Windows High Contrast / forced-colors. The "inherited from Fluent UI/FrontComposer" escape clause at `DESIGN.md:165` is correct but leaves QA without a test target, and status chips that rely on background fill lose meaning under forced-colors. *Fix:* add a row or sentence pinning the same minima (4.5:1 text, 3:1 non-text) explicitly to both light and dark, and stating that status-chip meaning must survive forced-colors via icon, text label, or border, not background fill alone.
- **low** Form validation rule (`EXPERIENCE.md:131`, `:204`) says "focus moved to summary" and "validation summary appears before fields" but does not require error association via `aria-describedby` from each invalid input to its message, nor `aria-invalid` on the field. *Fix:* add the standard error-association requirement to the Forms and validation row of Accessibility Floor or Tenant Configuration recovery.
- **low** The AI-vs-human distinction is handled visually via Actor badges and Risk chips, but the spine does not require the AI-actor distinction to be conveyed non-visually for every message. `EXPERIENCE.md:169` says "actor type, permitted identity label" is exposed per message but does not pin the order or require it precede the message text. *Fix:* add a sentence requiring the actor-type label to precede message content in the accessible name/description, so screen-reader users hear "AI" before they hear the proposal text.

## Strengths

- Keyboard and focus model is concrete and load-bearing: landmark list (`EXPERIENCE.md:159`), focus return on dialog close (`:160`), focus-on-error-summary for approval/association/correction/tenant config (`:162`), Escape rules that protect unsaved edits (`:163`), and the explicit rejection of tooltip-only disabled-control explanations (`:164`).
- Live-region discipline at the state-family level (`EXPERIENCE.md:124-134`) avoids the typical chatbot mistake of announcing every historical message on load. Skeleton loading uses `aria-busy` with no historical-message announcement (`:126`), and the "new updates" affordance pattern (`:134`, `:177`) protects users reading older history from forced scroll.
- Reduced-motion coverage is unusually thorough (`EXPERIENCE.md:175-181`): shimmer skeletons, row movement, streaming text animation, panel transitions, and queue row insertion all named, with text-status fallbacks ("Scanning attachment", "Projection pending") instead of motion-only progress.
- Fail-closed redaction (`EXPERIENCE.md:60`, `:105`, `:194`) consistently demands that redacted/unauthorized states remain understandable to screen-reader users "without leaking hidden content" - rare and correct.
- Confirmation discipline for irreversible AI actions (`EXPERIENCE.md:201`, Flow 8 `:339-348`) gives accessible explicit-confirmation copy a clear home and pairs every confirmation with audit visibility for the rejection/revision/cancel paths.
- Localization rules go beyond truncation: avoiding concatenated accessible names (`EXPERIENCE.md:220`), locale-aware pluralization (`:219`), and the column-collapse priority list (`:221`) make French expansion testable for QA.
- Small-screen fallback (`EXPERIENCE.md:233`) explicitly equalizes the screen-reader experience with the visual one - the limitation, remaining actions, and recovery path are reachable from the surface heading or first blocked-state panel, which is the right anchor for AT users.
- Contrast table in `DESIGN.md:154-163` covers the high-risk pairs (status chips, focus ring, code/identifier text, primary action) with explicit 4.5:1 / 3:1 minima rather than hand-waving at "Fluent defaults."
