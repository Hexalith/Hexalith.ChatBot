Hexalith.ChatBot — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28

The spine pair is broken as a current downstream contract despite strong structure and substantial behavioral detail. Its nine flows semantically cover the nine source journeys, all four frontmatter sources resolve, all 17 locally declared components pair across the spines, and visual-reference handling is explicit; however, source names and S1–S10 mappings are not preserved, the DESIGN color map violates the required token type, and current safety requirements for expired evidence and correction propagation are absent.

The specialist reviews reinforce that the original June accessibility and governance foundation is unusually strong, but later source changes did not reach the peer spines. Accessibility is thin for current governed-chat semantics, while governance and safety are broken by an unresolved approval-boundary contradiction and missing evidence-freshness, correction, bounded-admin, and informed-approval contracts.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Thin. The June accessibility foundation is concrete, but current governed-chat semantics require behavior that inheritance cannot supply. Findings: 0 critical, 5 high, 4 medium, 0 low.

high

Accessibility rules are split across unreferenced peers

DESIGN.md:4-11; EXPERIENCE.md:3-10; index.md:8-18
The spines predate the governed chat and later binding addenda, making behavior depend on discovery order.
Fix: Distill every binding accessibility delta into the peer spines.
high

AI summaries are not programmatically distinguished

prd.md:1198-1201,1469-1472
Generated interpretation may be heard as authoritative evidence.
Fix: Add semantic Source evidence and AI summary components with provenance-first reading order and keyboard disclosure.
high

Evidence-expiry behavior is not accessible

prd.md:1441
No accessible freshness state, transition announcement, focus preservation, or explained disabled approval exists.
Fix: Add timestamp/state association, forced-colors treatment, one-time announcements, and an aria-disabled reason.
high

Correction progress and delay lack accessible states

prd.md:450-451,1324-1325
Assistive-technology behavior is undefined during derived-store invalidation.
Fix: Define labels, progress semantics, deduplicated announcements, owner/next action, and focus preservation.
high

Incremental AI output lacks bounded announcements

EXPERIENCE.md:127-139,181,194-200
Streaming chunks could repeatedly interrupt screen-reader reading.
Fix: Keep streamed content outside live regions and announce only start/completion/stop/failure once.
medium

Classification and “why” disclosure lack accessible behavior

prd.md:1194-1201
Message association, labelled facts, reading order, expanded state, and focus return are unspecified.
Fix: Define both patterns in the peer spines.
medium

Mixed-language content lacks metadata

EXPERIENCE.md:224-230
English/French rules do not define page language or language of email, AI, and quoted content.
Fix: Bind root lang to locale, tag known language-of-parts, and test EN/FR screen readers.
medium

Focus can be obscured by authored chrome

DESIGN.md:164,180-190; EXPERIENCE.md:167-172
Focus order and contrast do not guarantee WCAG 2.2 Focus Not Obscured.
Fix: Require visible focused targets, scroll margins, and 200%/400% zoom tests.
medium

Association radiogroup behavior is undefined

implementation-conformance-addendum-2026-07-17.md:34-44
Selection, arrow navigation, validation, focus recovery, and refresh behavior remain inferred.
Fix: Define one named radiogroup plus a non-committing selection and explicit decision bar.

Governance & safety review

review-governance-safety.md

Broken. The pair is strongly fail-closed in its original scope, but one source contradiction can change whether boundary-crossing AI actions execute without approval, and later controls never reached the spines. Findings: 1 critical, 5 high, 3 medium, 0 low.

critical

Approval-boundary sources contradict each other

prd.md:1215-1237; addendum.md:25-33,65-74
The same six effect classes are both necessarily approval-required and tenant-downgradable to low-risk.
Fix: Approve one evaluation order and outcome taxonomy; require approval for all six until resolved.
high

Evidence freshness is not fail-closed

DESIGN.md:147,218-224; EXPERIENCE.md:75-81
Warning color exists without freshness data, expiry blocks, refresh, or audit behavior.
Fix: Require freshness everywhere evidence appears and block expired decisions with evidence-expired.
high

Correction may finish before derived context is safe

EXPERIENCE.md:98,114,293-301
The contract lacks store acknowledgement, delay handling, and AI-context blocking until invalidation completes.
Fix: Add correcting/delayed progress, owner/estimate/escalation, and a hard AI-action gate.
high

Bounded administration and two-person control are absent

EXPERIENCE.md:43,120,303-311
One admin can appear to activate sensitive policy, and queue visibility is not separated from project authority.
Fix: Add two-admin proposal/approval, conflict/rejection/expiry, activation, scope, justification, and audit states.
high

Denial copy can disclose resource existence

EXPERIENCE.md:52-61,106,111-116

high

Approval review lacks authority and effect detail

DESIGN.md:222-224; EXPERIENCE.md:79-81,334-343

medium

AI-summary provenance is missing

medium

EXPERIENCE.md:58-59,103-105,207-213
The UX lacks stable identity, prior-outcome reuse, conflict types, ceilings, and replay windows.
