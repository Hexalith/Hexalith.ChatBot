Hexalith.ChatBot — UX Design Validation Report

_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/ux-designs/ux-Hexalith.ChatBot-2026-05-28

The spine pair is structurally disciplined and unusually complete at the surface, component, accessibility, and visual-inheritance levels, but it is not yet safe to finalize as the downstream implementation contract. Source-to-spine drift in canonical workflow states and stale safety-governance language could cause architecture and story authors to implement behavior that differs from the current PRD and addendum.

Additional lenses reinforce the result: accessibility is broadly strong but the larger-screen handoff conflicts with WCAG 2.2 AA reflow; Fluent conformance is partial because several canonical bases are unavailable or ambiguous and accordion ownership is underspecified; the persisted governance review identifies unsafe ambiguity in batching, queue authority, suppressed candidates, and correction timing.

Accessibility review

review-accessibility.md

Adequate overall, with one high-impact reflow contradiction and two operational accessibility gaps.

high

Larger-screen handoff contradicts WCAG 2.2 AA Reflow

DESIGN.md:149-155 · EXPERIENCE.md:21-23,255-263
The handoff can remove required information/actions at 320 CSS pixels or 400% zoom.
Fix: Make handoff optional, linearize workflows, and test every M1/M2 surface at 320px and 400% zoom.
medium

Operational auto-refresh lacks user control

EXPERIENCE.md:104-116,169-205,240
Queues/dashboards can silently reorder or remove active context.
Fix: Queue updates behind an accessible apply action or pause control and preserve focus/active row.
medium

Disconnected and response-lost submissions are unspecified

EXPERIENCE.md:83-116,138-194
Governed actions lack accessible not-sent, pending/unknown, and prior-outcome recovery states.
Fix: Preserve drafts/focus, retain operation identity, announce connectivity, and reconcile status before retry.
