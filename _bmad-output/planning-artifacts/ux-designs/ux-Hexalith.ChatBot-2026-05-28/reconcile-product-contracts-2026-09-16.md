## Conflicts resolved

1. **Draft/STOP language vs approved product source.** The old UX snapshot treated product approval as open. The final PRD and approved addendum, plus the approved sprint change proposal, resolve document authority. The UX blocker is now release qualification/downstream conformance, not product-document approval.
2. **Correction surface ambiguity.** The PRD places `correct` on M0 S2 and introduces S4 in M1 (`prd.md:510-521`). The durable decision at `.memlog.md:17` resolves the presentation: M0 S2 owns inline entry/status and durable detail; S1 is deep-link only; S4 is the M1 extension. A correction modal is rejected.
3. **Generic low-risk AI language at M0.** The exact v0 allowlist limits M0 to approved `Project.AppendConversationMessage`; the low-risk assistance command begins in M1. The spines no longer imply that M0 may execute autonomous read-only AI help.
4. **Loose readiness/status language.** “Pass,” “approved,” file presence, prose approval, and local booleans no longer imply qualification. Exact gate records and the published gate set govern, but ordinary Project surfaces do not expose raw set identifiers; dashboard and health labels use their distinct closed vocabularies.
5. **Product brief scope vs final increments.** The brief's generic email, user upload, schedule/file triggers, broad task automation, subject workspace, and same-core-command MVP language are not imported as current MVP scope where the final PRD narrows, stages, or omits them.
