## Product-intent and qualitative reconciliation

### Email-first collaboration remains the wedge

The PRD still leads with external project work happening through email and keeps external participants represented as tenant-scoped Parties without requiring authentication or another portal. The new authenticity and untrusted-content contracts are internal safety behavior; they do not burden Elena's external-email journey or add a customer-facing security workflow.

### The user promise remains legible

The original promise—collaborate simply around a project or subject, with AI moving work forward without losing context—remains quoted in §Executive Summary. “Subject” still resolves to a governed Project for actionable MVP work. The new states and evidence fields stay subordinate to the journey value moments:

- Amira retains controlled acceleration and plain-language approval/refusal behavior.
- Marc retains evidence-led association review, including deliberate deferral.
- Elena retains invisible governance through ordinary email.
- Priya's repair journey now delivers accountable correction across every affected owner and irreversible effect.
- Nora and Leo retain operational confidence and repeatability without bypass paths.

### Cross-surface parity remains a product capability, not equal privilege

The split between `mcp-human-delegated-client` and AI/tool MCP principals preserves the original same-command-model promise. Human-delegated MCP sessions can exercise actor-authorized parity operations with current user presence; AI/tool/service principals cannot approve their own or another AI proposal. This is consistent with the brief's human-approval promise and does not remove legitimate CLI/MCP capability.

## Scope and ownership reconciliation

The M0 → M1 → M2 order remains intact. M0 is still the UI-only controlled preview, M1 the governed cross-surface pilot, and M2 the production release-candidate increment. Scheduled/file-addition/broad-event triggers, general uploads, additional channels, unrestricted commands, full task lifecycle, broad document intelligence, commercial packaging, and non-Project workspaces remain outside MVP.

The pre-pilot `O1` data-rights operations API/provisioning interface is scoped as a restricted compliance prerequisite rather than a general UI, CLI/MCP parity surface, or external-participant portal. It closes an operability hole without restoring deferred breadth.

ChatBot still owns orchestration and security-sensitive derived records. Projects, Conversations, Folders, Parties, Tenants, EventStore, and mail integration retain their original source authority. The correction impact manifest makes this boundary more explicit: ChatBot coordinates owner commands and waits for authenticated acknowledgements; it does not directly mutate source conversations/files or claim that irreversible effects were undone.
