The product thesis, named journeys, staged scope, measurable outcomes, and non-negotiable trust floor are unusually substantive. The current PRD/addendum pair is not safe to use as the sole implementation or release authority, however: two binding AI-governance rules permit readings that contradict mandatory approval and least privilege, while late chat-surface and recovery changes have not been reconciled through the full requirement set. Treat the product strategy as sound but the requirements contract as blocked until the critical and high findings below are resolved.

The adversarial reviewer independently confirmed the stop decision and widened the risk picture. It additionally found that the post-commit audit sequence cannot satisfy the fail-closed invariant and that native-store tenant-isolation proof arrives after affected stores and programmable surfaces ship. Across both reviews there are 6 Critical, 17 High, 9 Medium, and 1 Low reports before deduplication; the Critical reports describe four unique blockers.

Decision-readiness

broken

The PRD states real decisions and trade-offs, including the email wedge, fixed M0/M1/M2 order, deferred machine parity, and protected safety floor. It cannot support an unambiguous green-light decision because approval policy may waive mandatory controls, the M1 AI allowlist is unsafe by default, and M2 release obligations lack complete evidence and SLO definitions.

FR41; NFR16; addendum §Tenant Policy Schema
