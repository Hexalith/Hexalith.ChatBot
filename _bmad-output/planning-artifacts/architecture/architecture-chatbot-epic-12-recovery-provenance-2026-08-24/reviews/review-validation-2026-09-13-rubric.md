## Verdict

**NOT VALID AS A CURRENT BUILD SUBSTRATE:** the current-run, exact-path, single-writer trust split remains sound, but one critical enforcement gap and three high-severity brownfield contradictions mean the final 2026-08-24 spine no longer describes the evidence pipeline that exists on 2026-09-13.

## Findings

### R-001 — Critical — The completion gate is not protected, so “required check” is not an enforced invariant

- **Evidence:** AD-2 says every producer, cleanup, attestation, or validation failure leaves “the required check” failed (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:51-55`). The repository's own TE-2 record says completion remains blocked on required-check activation and marks TE-2.5 in progress (`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/technical-enablers.md:34-52`); sprint workflow notes repeat that no subsequent story may reach `done` until the check is required (`_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/sprint-status.yaml:30-37`); and the developer guide says the external control is still absent (`docs/story-evidence-integrity.md:3-5`). Current GitHub configuration confirms the gap: `gh api repos/Hexalith/Hexalith.ChatBot/branches/main/protection` returned HTTP 404 `Branch not protected`, while `gh api repos/Hexalith/Hexalith.ChatBot/rules/branches/main` and `gh api repos/Hexalith/Hexalith.ChatBot/rulesets` both returned `[]` on 2026-09-13.
- **Architectural consequence:** the repository can emit a red `story-evidence-integrity` result, but no merge/direct-push control prevents a completion transition from landing anyway. AD-2 therefore does not prevent its stated delivery-integrity divergence at the DevOps/repository-administrator seam. The spine's operational/environmental envelope is silent about the only external control that makes the evidence pipeline authoritative.
- **Recommended disposition:** **defer** only as an explicit open operational dependency: name the protected branches, required check name, DevOps owner, current inactive state, and closure evidence in the spine. Until activation is verified, replace claims that the check is already required with “must be required” and do not treat this spine as final/enforced.

### R-002 — High — Completion artifacts now cross the trust boundary that AD-2 and AD-4 prohibit

- **Evidence:** the spine models a single post-validation retention flow (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:28-40,51-67`) and says completion-run operational reports/manifests are diagnostics-only, not uploaded, and not eligible for A10 (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:63-67`). The memlog records the same decision (`.memlog.md:18`). Current code writes the operational bundle under repository-root `TestResults/live-recovery/<runId>` (`tests/Hexalith.ChatBot.IntegrationTests/Recovery/LiveContinuityAspireE2eTests.cs:67-85`), stamps its manifests with `artifact:completion-recovery-evidence` (`LiveContinuityAspireE2eTests.cs:707-723`), and writes report/manifest files with locators for `/results.trx` and `/reports` (`FileRecoveryValidationEvidenceSink.cs:220-271,301-325`). The workflow then uploads the entire `TestResults` tree as `completion-recovery-evidence` before the `ci` attestation/validation command runs (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:349-395,438-455`). A second artifact, `story-evidence-integrity-reports`, later retains the canonical recovery TRX, sidecar, and gate report. The declared developer guide and companion ADR still claim the operational side-products are outside every upload and that retention follows attestation/validation (`docs/story-evidence-integrity.md:112-118`; `docs/adrs/live-recovery-validation-drivers.md:133-139`).
- **Architectural consequence:** workflow, recovery-harness, evidence-gate, and A10 reviewers now see two completion-run archives with overlapping content but no spine-level ownership or authority rule. Operational reports explicitly advertise the pre-validation archive even though AD-4 says they do not exist there; the sanitized canonical TRX is also externally retained before its TE-2 sidecar exists. Raw TRX remains outside upload, so no raw-payload leak was found, but a downstream consumer can still mistake a pre-attestation diagnostic bundle for completion evidence or cite completion-run operational manifests as A10 evidence.
- **Recommended disposition:** **discuss** and choose one contract. Either suppress completion-run operational publication and retain only after TE-2 validation, or explicitly add a separate pre-validation diagnostic artifact with a closed metadata schema, truthful layout, non-authoritative label, owner, retention/failure rules, and an executable prohibition on its use by TE-2 retained evidence or the A10 gate. Reconcile the spine, memlog, workflow, developer guide, ADR, and architecture document together.

### R-003 — High — The completion toolchain has two version owners and the signed topology version has moved

- **Evidence:** the spine binds the exact-head completion stack to .NET SDK `10.0.302` (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:85-96`), and every CI setup step still requests `10.0.302` (for example `.github/workflows/ci.yml:173-176`). The repository's authoritative `global.json` now requires `10.0.400` with `rollForward: latestPatch` (`global.json:1-6`), and the current architecture test asserts `10.0.400` (`tests/Hexalith.ChatBot.Architecture.Tests/ScaffoldArchitectureTests.cs:726-738`). Consequently, the setup action and SDK resolver no longer identify the same compiler/toolchain; success can depend on what the hosted runner image already contains. In the same producer boundary, the live class composes the AppHost now built with `Aspire.AppHost.Sdk/13.5.3` (`src/Hexalith.ChatBot.AppHost/Hexalith.ChatBot.AppHost.csproj:1`), while Story 12.15 and the companion architecture/ADR still describe the ratified resource-command topology as Aspire `13.4.6` (`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/architecture.md:309-317`; `docs/adrs/live-recovery-validation-drivers.md:13-17`). The spine does not name Aspire in its completion stack at all.
- **Architectural consequence:** the implementation digest can be tested with a compiler or Aspire composition version different from the version the spine and setup step claim. The global build owner, CI owner, AppHost owner, and evidence reviewer can each make locally reasonable changes that no longer converge; an SDK/topology compatibility failure either blocks every completion or, worse for provenance, makes the selected toolchain depend on mutable runner inventory rather than the repository.
- **Recommended disposition:** **autofix** by establishing one version authority (the repository baseline already makes `global.json` authoritative), deriving CI setup from it or testing exact equality, and updating the spine/companions to the actual AppHost SDK after the dedicated technology lens verifies fit. Add Aspire to the completion stack or explicitly inherit it from one named authoritative control file.

### R-004 — High — AD-5's exact safety ladder is stale and would regress the current cleanup design if followed

- **Evidence:** AD-5 and its memlog decision bind a `265`-minute in-process deadline and `280`-minute live step (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:69-73`; `.memlog.md:19`). The current completion workflow deliberately uses a `250`-minute in-process deadline, a dynamically bounded external SIGINT capped at `265` minutes, and a `285`-minute GitHub step (`.github/workflows/ci.yml:285-320`). Its comment explains that equal inner/outer bounds made the in-process cleanup guard unreachable (`ci.yml:292-295`), while the live class budgets ten serial scenarios against the 250-minute minimum (`tests/Hexalith.ChatBot.IntegrationTests/Recovery/LiveContinuityAspireE2eTests.cs:201-223`). `docs/story-evidence-integrity.md:116` and the companion ADR at `docs/adrs/live-recovery-validation-drivers.md:135` still repeat the superseded `265/280` model.
- **Architectural consequence:** AD-5's goal—a fixed minute-330 closeout and final cleanup/publication reserve—is still implemented, but its enforceable Rule no longer ratifies the brownfield system. A workflow maintainer following the final spine can collapse the restored 15-minute separation between in-process cancellation and external interruption, recreating the half-injected-fault/cleanup race the current code was changed to prevent.
- **Recommended disposition:** **autofix** the memlog and re-distilled spine to ratify the current `250 in-process / 265 SIGINT / 285 step / 330 closeout / 360 job` ladder, including which owner may tune each value and the invariant inequalities that tests must enforce. Reconcile the guide and ADR in the same update.

### R-005 — Medium — A known freshness-override provenance gap is absent from Deferred

- **Evidence:** policy v2.1 gives named primary-lane bindings a 360-minute current-run window while leaving the global default at 60 minutes (`story-evidence-policy.json:4-6,78-190`). `ProvenanceAttestor` resolves that override from the contract-author-controlled `lane` string (`tools/Hexalith.ChatBot.StoryEvidenceGate/ProvenanceAttestor.cs:98-113`), but `StoryEvidenceValidator` binds lane identity to policy only for declared required primary classes (`StoryEvidenceValidator.cs:313-360`), and the standalone `attest` command does not run `CompletionProductionPlanner` (`Program.cs:85-95`). This is already recorded as open `DW-124`, with a concrete closure test, in `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/deferred-work.md:1067-1073`; the spine's Deferred section does not mention it (`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:108-113`). The exact CI planning path does reject a falsely named `recovery-primary` lane, which limits the exposure, but the documented local `attest` + `validate` path remains weaker.
- **Architectural consequence:** two contract authors can receive different freshness semantics from the same policy by choosing different lane names, and local preflight can accept up to six-hour-old evidence under the privileged name without the corresponding recovery trigger. Because protected-check enforcement is currently absent (R-001), this cannot be dismissed as harmless local ergonomics. The omission also violates the spine rule that real unresolved divergence points must be visibly Deferred rather than living only in a separate ledger.
- **Recommended disposition:** **defer** visibly in this spine with the existing DW-124 owner, risk, and closure evidence, or fix by allowlisting/binding every lane that carries an age override regardless of `requiredClasses` and exercising the same validation in standalone attestation.

## Confirmed strengths

- `CompletionProductionPlanner` still rejects retained/alternate-path/second-consumer recovery declarations before DAPR and validates the exact source, selector, path, locator, and primary-class binding.
- `ProvenanceAttestor` remains the sole TE-2 sidecar writer and preflights current-run inputs before writing; raw recovery TRX remains under runner temporary storage and outside artifact upload.
- Read-only `contents`/`actions` permissions, checksum-pinned Dapr CLI/runtime 1.18.0, current artifact-action majors as declared in the spine, exact event-head checks, and fail-closed producer/cleanup behavior remain present.
- The explicit Deferred entries for retained completion, post-upload artifact identity, hosted duration evidence, and A10 authenticity remain appropriately bounded; none silently creates a second completion path.

R-002 needs an architecture choice about diagnostic versus authoritative artifacts. R-003 and R-004 are clear brownfield reconciliation updates. Carry R-005 into Deferred unless it is fixed in the same change, then rerun lint and all configured reviewer lenses against the re-distilled spine.
